They also rebuilt and maintained the company website and WordPress blog, taking over updates, backups and uptime.

They configured firewalls, deployed an intelligent CCTV system, and set up Starlink connectivity and web-managed printers. Day to day they handled first and second line support, from BitLocker recovery and missing drivers to hardware repair, and wrote up recurring fixes so the next one took minutes, not hours.
