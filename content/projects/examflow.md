## Why

Assessing students on internship meant reports, supervisor feedback and grades passing between three people. The portal puts all of it online.

## Approach

Built in PHP with MySQL on the MVC pattern. Supervisors assign and grade tasks, students write weekly reports that the supervisor approves before they reach the assessor, and supervisors can follow each student's progress and message the assessor. Role-based access keeps each person to their own view.
